Last updated: May 2026

1. What CafeSnap is (and what we are not)

CafeSnap is an independent, automated reservation assistance service. We are a bot, not a human concierge. Our software monitors the Pokemon Cafe website and attempts to secure a reservation on your behalf when booking windows open or when a cancellation slot appears. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to The Pokemon Company, Nintendo, or Pokemon Cafe in any way.

The reservation at the cafe itself is free. You are paying for our automation service only. The booking is made in your own name and is yours to attend.

2. Pricing

We charge a flat fee per booking request, regardless of party size (up to 6 guests per reservation at both cafes). The price is shown at checkout in your selected currency. This is a one-time fee. There are no hidden charges and no per-person fees.

3. Refund if we cannot secure your booking

If we have not secured a reservation for you by the day after the last of your preferred dates passes, we automatically issue a full refund to your original payment method. You do not need to ask for it. (If you only picked one date, the refund happens the day after that date passes. We keep scanning for cancellation slots right up to that point.)

Refunds reach your bank within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your card issuer. You receive an email confirmation the moment the refund is sent.

4. Cancelling your booking

You can cancel any time on the Check booking page. If you cancel before we have secured your seat, a processing fee of 10% (about £1.50 equivalent, charged in your own currency) is deducted from your refund to cover payment-processing costs. The rest is returned to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Once we have confirmed your reservation at the cafe, the service has been delivered and the fee is non-refundable. You can still cancel through us up to the deadline below so the seat is released and does not go to waste: we will automatically attempt to cancel your seat on the Pokemon Cafe website, but no money is returned for a booking we already secured. Once the cafe slot has been released, please do not attend, since your seat will have passed to the next customer in the cafe's queue.

Cancellation deadline: cancellations must be submitted by 10pm JST the night before your reservation date. After that, the cafe no longer accepts cancellations through their system, and the seat is held in your name.

5. 14-day cooling-off period waiver

By paying, you expressly request that we begin providing the service immediately, and you acknowledge that you waive your statutory 14-day cooling-off right under the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. The service is performed immediately upon payment: our bot starts attempting to book within seconds. The cancellation terms in section 4 apply instead.

6. No guarantee of specific seats or times

We do our best to book one of your preferred time slots, but we cannot guarantee a specific time, seat, or seating zone. Reservations are subject to real-time availability at the cafe. We will book the best available match from your selected preferences.

Your name and email are submitted to the Pokemon Cafe booking form as part of placing the reservation. We do not share, sell, or use your information for marketing.
